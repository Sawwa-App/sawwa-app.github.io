# 1. About Sawwa
Sawwa ("we", "us") is an app run during the pilot by the Sawwa team in Egypt. Sawwa is not a registered company yet, which is one reason it charges nothing during the pilot. By creating an account or using Sawwa you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

# 2. Sawwa is a platform only
1. Sawwa is a technology platform that helps people who are already making the same trip find each other and share its costs (carpooling).
2. Sawwa is not a transport company, taxi service or ride-hailing operator. We do not own or operate vehicles, employ drivers, or provide transport.
3. Each trip is a private arrangement between the driver and the riders. The driver is solely responsible for the vehicle and the trip.
4. Drivers are not our employees, agents or contractors, and they must not use Sawwa to make a profit or as a job.

# 3. No payments through Sawwa
1. Sawwa does not collect, hold or process any payments, and does not charge riders or drivers any fee or commission during the pilot.
2. The seat price shown in the app is a suggested share of the trip's fuel and running costs, capped so the driver does not profit. Riders pay it directly to the driver, in cash or as they agree.
3. Sawwa is not a party to any payment between users and is not responsible for unpaid, late or disputed amounts.
4. If we introduce fees in the future, we will tell you at least 30 days in advance, and you will need to accept updated terms to continue.

# 4. Who can use Sawwa
1. You must be at least 18 years old and have an Egyptian mobile number with WhatsApp.
2. You sign in by sending a one-time code from your WhatsApp to Sawwa's number. You must only use your own number.
3. You must give true information, including your name and gender. Gender is used for ladies-only rides and can only be changed by Sawwa.
4. You are responsible for everything done through your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else is using it.

# 5. Drivers
1. To offer rides you must be approved by Sawwa. You must upload a selfie, the front and back of your national ID, your driving license, and the car license for each car you use.
2. You must hold a valid driving license, and your car must be licensed and roadworthy.
3. You must follow Egyptian traffic laws, drive safely, and never drive under the influence of drugs or alcohol.
4. You must not ask riders for more than the seat price shown in the app.
5. Approval is not a guarantee by Sawwa of any driver's skill, conduct or vehicle.

# 6. Riders
1. You must respect the driver and other riders, and wear a seat belt.
2. You must pay the driver the agreed seat price.

# 7. Ladies-only rides
Women drivers may make their rides ladies-only. Only women riders can see and book these rides. Misstating your gender to use them is a serious breach of these terms and leads to a permanent ban of your phone number.

# 8. Cancellations and strikes
1. Riders and drivers can cancel for free up to 12 hours before departure.
2. A cancellation within 12 hours, or not showing up, counts as a strike.
3. Three strikes within 90 days suspend the account for 14 days. The same rules apply to drivers and riders.
4. A published ride that does not start within 30 minutes of its departure time is cancelled automatically, and the driver gets a strike if any rider was confirmed on it.

# 9. Safety
1. Check the driver, car and plate in the app before you get in.
2. In an emergency call the police (122) or ambulance (123) first. Sawwa does not provide emergency services.
3. Report any unsafe behaviour to us through Contact support in the app. Our team reviews reports, which can take several days, especially during the pilot. Never rely on it when you are in danger.

# 10. Your data and location
1. We store your phone number, name, gender, profile details, trips and, for drivers, verification documents. Documents are stored encrypted and only reviewed by Sawwa staff.
2. During a trip, the driver's app shares the driver's location with the riders, including when the app is in the background.
3. Riders can share their location with the driver from 30 minutes before pickup until pickup. This live location is not saved.
4. Phone numbers are shown only between a driver and a confirmed rider of the same trip.
5. We use Google Maps and similar services to show maps, search places and calculate routes.
6. We do not sell your data. See our [PRIVACY POLICY LINK] for details.
